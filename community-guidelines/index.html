Beacon Safety

Community Guidelines

Clear rules, visible controls, and a defined response for keeping Beacon's communities safe.

Effective date 30 August 2026 Safety contact contactbeacon.kr@gmail.com

01

Our standard

Beacon is for useful, respectful community participation. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. These rules apply to profiles, posts, comments, messages, media, groups, events, and other content or behavior on Beacon.

02

What is not allowed

  • Harassment and hate: threats, bullying, stalking, degrading attacks, or hateful conduct based on a protected or personal characteristic.
  • Sexual and exploitative content: pornography, non-consensual intimate material, sexual solicitation, or any sexual content involving minors.
  • Violence and self-harm: credible threats, graphic glorification, instructions to harm others, or content encouraging suicide or self-harm.
  • Privacy and impersonation: doxxing, exposing private data, pretending to be another person, or deceptive identity claims.
  • Illegal or harmful activity: fraud, malware, illegal goods, exploitation, or instructions that create a serious risk to people or property.
  • Spam and manipulation: repetitive promotion, scams, coordinated disruption, or attempts to evade safety controls and enforcement.

03

Filtering before publication

Beacon checks text submitted to community surfaces and rejects known abusive or objectionable patterns before publication. The server also enforces safety checks on protected content tables. Automated filtering cannot understand every context, so reports and human review remain essential.

04

How to report content or behavior

Open the menu for the relevant post, comment, message, profile, or event and select Report. Choose the closest reason and add factual context. The reported user does not see the reporter's identity, but authorized Beacon moderators can access the report to investigate and prevent misuse.

If the in-app control is unavailable, email contactbeacon.kr@gmail.com with a link or screenshot, the reason for the report, and the email address connected to your Beacon account. Do not send your password.

05

How blocking works

Select Report & Block User from an abusive user's content. Beacon immediately hides that user's posts and messages from your feed and creates a moderation report so the developer is notified. You can view and unblock users from Profile → Blocked Users.

Blocking protects your view of Beacon. It does not replace a report when a user or content may threaten other people, so the combined Report & Block action performs both steps.

06

Moderation response commitment

Beacon reviews reports of objectionable content within 24 hours. When a violation is confirmed, Beacon removes the offending content and ejects or suspends the user who provided it. Serious or urgent reports are prioritized, and repeat or severe abuse may result in immediate account termination.

A report may be closed without enforcement when the content does not violate these rules, lacks enough information to identify the target, or is submitted to harass another user. Beacon records moderation actions needed to operate the safety process.

07

Appeals, safety questions, and emergencies

To ask about a moderation decision, email contactbeacon.kr@gmail.com from your account email and include the content or report reference. Beacon reviews appeals separately from the original decision where practical.

Beacon is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service first, then report the content to Beacon when it is safe to do so.
