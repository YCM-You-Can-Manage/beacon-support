01

Scope and operator

This Privacy Policy applies to the Beacon mobile application, Beacon support pages, and related community services. Beacon is a community app for people living in Korea, including students, workers, newcomers, and long-term residents.

Questions and privacy requests can be sent to contactbeacon.kr@gmail.com.

02

Information we handle

Account and profile information

Beacon requires the information needed to create and operate an account, such as name, email address, country, user status, and account identifiers. Display name, profile image, gender, date of birth, university, and community affiliation are optional profile information unless a clearly identified feature needs a specific field. Gender and date of birth are not required to create an account, sign in, or use Beacon's core community features.

Verification information

Information and files submitted to verify an account, such as student or community details and, where required, an identity document or passport image. Verification information is not displayed as public profile content.

Community and user content

Communities joined, membership requests, posts, comments, group messages, reports, uploaded photos or videos, saved content, events, and calendar participation.

Device and notification information

Push-notification tokens and related device, operating system, app version, language, and time-zone information needed to deliver and manage notifications.

Support and technical information

Information you include when contacting support, together with limited diagnostic or service-reliability information generated by the app and its service providers.

03

How we use information

  • Create, authenticate, and manage Beacon accounts.
  • Verify users and protect the relevance of communities.
  • Provide posts, groups, conversations, events, and calendars.
  • Send requested service and community notifications.
  • Review reports and enforce community and safety rules.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and account requests.
  • Secure, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve Beacon.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Beacon does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for cross-app advertising tracking.

04

Service providers and sharing

Beacon uses service providers to operate the app. These currently include:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, real-time features, server functions, and file storage.
  • Google Firebase for app configuration and push notification delivery.

Information may also be shared when you choose to publish it to a community, when necessary to protect users and the service, or when disclosure is required by applicable law. Public profile and community content may be visible to other authorized Beacon users according to the relevant community settings.

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Beacon takes reasonable steps to use providers that apply appropriate security and privacy safeguards.

05

Retention and deletion

Beacon retains information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security, resolve reports and disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention can vary depending on the information and why it is processed.

Users can initiate permanent account deletion directly inside the app at Profile → Delete Account. After password confirmation, Beacon deletes the authentication account and associated account data and stored user files. Content may be de-identified instead of deleted only where needed to protect other users, investigate abuse, comply with law, or establish or defend legal claims.

Users can also complete the same verified deletion process on Beacon’s secure account deletion website. The website requires the account email and current password and deletes only the user identified by that signed session.

06

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

  • Update available profile information inside Beacon.
  • Change notification permissions in Beacon or device settings.
  • Manage optional policy preferences in the app.
  • Delete your account through the in-app flow or the account deletion website.
  • Email contactbeacon.kr@gmail.com for access, correction, privacy, or deletion assistance.

Beacon is intended for users who can lawfully create an account and consent to personal-information processing. Where consent from a parent or legal guardian is required, users must not create an account without that consent.

07

Security

Beacon uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information. Verification files are stored separately from public community content, and access controls are used to limit access to sensitive information. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

08

Changes to this policy

Beacon may update this Privacy Policy when its services, data practices, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or through another appropriate channel.
