Before you continue

This cannot be undone.

Beacon authenticates directly with Supabase. This website never stores your password and cannot choose another user's account.

  1. Authenticate. Your email and password establish a signed session.
  2. Re-verify. The deletion service checks the password again for that same signed-in user.
  3. Delete. Only the authenticated user ID and its associated data and files are removed.
What is removed

Your authentication account, profile, memberships, private account data, and user-owned files are deleted. Limited de-identified safety or legal records may be retained only when required to protect users or comply with law.
