Frequently asked

Answers without the wait.

If your question is not listed here, email Beacon Support and include enough detail for us to reproduce the issue.

Who can use Beacon?

Beacon is a community app for people living in Korea. It is not limited to students; workers, newcomers, and long-term residents can also create an account and join relevant communities.

Why does Beacon verify accounts?

Verification helps keep communities relevant and trustworthy. The verification method depends on the user and community. Verification files are handled separately from public profile content.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

On the Beacon sign-in screen, choose “Forgot password?” and follow the instructions sent to your account email. Check your spam folder if the message does not arrive.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Open the menu on the relevant post, comment, user, or event and choose Report. Add context when requested so the moderation team can review the issue.

How do I manage notifications?

Open Profile and select Notifications to update Beacon notification preferences. Device-level notification permission can also be changed in iOS or Android settings.

How do I delete my Beacon account?

In Beacon, open Profile → Privacy Policy → Delete Account. Review the confirmation and complete the deletion request. If the option does not work, contact Beacon Support from the email address connected to your account.

Account controls

You can delete your account in the app.

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Select Privacy Policy.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review and confirm the request.
